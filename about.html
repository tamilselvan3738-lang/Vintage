Chronology

The Mechanical Evolution

Tracing our trajectory from intimate local assembly tables to state-of-the-art multi-room calibration sanctuaries.

ERA I — 2012

The Basement Transcription Lathe

Founded inside a small Brooklyn industrial loft by two audio recording technicians obsessively restoring broadcast-grade idler-drive platforms. Our custom high-mass slate isolation frames quickly established a dedicated regional following.

ERA II — 2016

Precision Tonearm Geometry Labs

Transitioning from restoration to custom original manufacturing. Launching our flagship low-friction fluid-damped unipivot tonearm mechanisms engineered to preserve delicate inner-groove tracking geometry perfectly without phase degradation.

ERA III — 2021

The Acoustic Vault Outposts

Inaugurating dedicated concrete-anchored zero-vibration evaluation suites. Integrating real-time vacuum tube pre-amplification arrays to let master disc evaluators audition hardware responses over pristine silent room floor limits.

Absolute Standards

Fundamental Design Doctrines

Every turntable setup and custom plinth build executed under our seal strictly obeys three unyielding acoustics guidelines.

01 Zero Phase Smearing

Absolute protection of the original stereo image timing. Eliminating superfluous line filters and circuit switches to pass sub-millivolt output waveforms directly from stylus cantilever to master amplification paths.

02 Uncompromising Plinth Inertia

Mechanical decoupling via multi-tier constrained layer damping. Utilizing cast magnesium base plates alongside high density composite core fills to isolate delicate tracking paths from low-end speaker feedback.

03 Tactile Mechanical Survival

Engineered for multi-generational analog enjoyment. Eschewing non-repairable integrated logic chips in favor of discrete, heavy-duty physical motor controllers and high torque analog bearing wells.
